A thin voice layer that stays out of your way

Jarvis routes and relays. The work, the tools, and the memory stay inside each agent's own harness.

  • Your Voice Only

    Every sentence is checked against your enrolled voiceprint locally with sherpa-onnx. TV, family, and background voices are dropped before reaching any model.

  • Right Agent, Every Time

    Coding tasks route to T3 Code by default, or to Claude Code and OpenCode when you name them. Everything else routes seamlessly to Hermes Agent.

  • Answers When You Mean It

    Zero wake word. A fast intent classifier tells speech aimed at Jarvis from ambient conversation around the room in roughly 230 milliseconds.

  • One-Line Digests

    Agents write 200-line reports. Jarvis reads the wall for you, speaks the outcome in about ten words, and reads pending approvals word for word.

  • Approvals Stay Yours

    Database migrations, destructive commands, and architecture choices pause until you say "yes". No model ever auto-approves on your behalf.

Built on Pipecat. Local where it counts.

Speech recognition and speaker verification run on your Mac's GPU and CPU. Only your verified sentences leave the machine.

Run jarvis init (again later: jarvis enroll) and speak three natural sentences. Jarvis extracts a local speaker embedding vector with sherpa-onnx so only your voice can trigger or interrupt agents.

A long coding run ends in a massive wall of diffs and one blocking permission prompt. Instead of babysitting the terminal, Jarvis summarizes the outcome and relays your voice "yes" from the kitchen.

Audio frames pass through echo cancellation, MLX Whisper on Apple Silicon, cosine voiceprint verification, and a 230ms executive intent filter before dispatching to T3 Code or Hermes.

Open named repositories under ~/Projects, replace running tasks mid-flight, or tell Jarvis to stay quiet during a call and brief you when you're back.

Three gates between your mic and your agents

Every spoken phrase is verified locally and filtered for intent so your agents only act when you talk to them.

  • 1. On-Device Acoustic Gate

    Echo cancellation strips Jarvis's own voice, MLX Whisper transcribes on Apple Silicon, and sherpa-onnx checks your speaker embedding locally.

    0 keypresses

  • 2. 230ms Executive Filter

    A fast decision model distinguishes instructions meant for Jarvis from side conversations in the room without requiring a rigid wake word.

    2 sentences max

  • 3. Multi-Harness Bridge

    Dispatches coding tasks to T3 Code over WebSocket, routes named runs to Claude Code or OpenCode, and hands general queries to Hermes Agent.

    Any room range
